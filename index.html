Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what people ask first. Every answer should be reviewed by an attorney before publication.

What should I do if I discover a construction defect?

Document the problem with dated photos, avoid repairs that destroy evidence where possible, and keep all related contracts and messages. Then speak with a lawyer, because the right steps depend on your facts.

What documents should I gather before speaking with a lawyer?

Contracts, change orders, invoices, payment records, plans, inspection reports, photographs, and correspondence with the builder or contractor.

Are there deadlines for bringing a construction-related claim in Texas?

Yes, time limits can apply and vary by claim type and circumstances. If you think a deadline may be close, say so in your first message.

What happens during an initial consultation?

You explain the situation, we ask follow-up questions, and we discuss whether and how we can help. We’ll also explain next steps and what information we still need.

Can a construction dispute be resolved without going to trial?

Many disputes resolve through negotiation, mediation, or arbitration, though that depends on the contract and the parties.
